Neyim KaldıTürkçe

Know what's at home, what's running low and what's about to expire

Neyim Kaldı ("What do I have left?") is an Android home inventory app for Turkish households. It keeps track of your groceries, expiry dates and shopping list, so in the supermarket you check your phone instead of wondering whether there's milk at home.

Coming soon to Google Play. The app is in Turkish.

Add without typingTake a photo of your shopping bag or receipt and the items appear in a list. Saying "3 milk, 2 bread" or scanning a barcode works too.
Reminders before things expireYou get a notification before the yogurt expires or the detergent runs out. Items running low go straight to the shopping list.
Online ordersOrders from Migros, Getir and Trendyol Go are added to your stock once you approve them.
What can I cook?Recipe ideas and a weekly dinner plan based on what you have. Missing ingredients go to the list.
Spending and pricesSee your spending by category and follow an item's price over months in TRY, Euro or Dollar.
Household items and roomsKeep clothes, furniture and devices by room. Get a reminder before a warranty ends.

Your data

No account needed. Your records stay on your phone in an encrypted database. For the AI features, photos and text are sent to Google Gemini. The privacy policy lists exactly what goes where.

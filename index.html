Neyim Kaldı

An Android app for keeping track of the food and household supplies you have at home and when they expire. Whatever runs low goes on your shopping list. The name means "what do I have left" in Turkish.

Android · Coming soon to Google Play

The app is in Turkish and made for households in Turkey.

At the store I could never remember what was at home, and I kept buying the same things twice. That's why I started writing Neyim Kaldı. I build it on my own, and for now it's Android only and in Turkish.

Abdullah Ak

Items and expiry dates

Put each item in a room and a cupboard. When the yogurt gets close to its date or the detergent runs low, the list says so next to it.

Empty the shopping bag on the table and take one photo. Google Gemini picks out each item, and you check the list and fix mistakes before anything is saved. You can also add from a receipt, a barcode or by voice.

Shopping list

When the milk drops below the amount you set, it goes on the list by itself. Add the rest yourself, then send the list over WhatsApp to whoever is going to the store.

Online orders

If you order from Migros, Getir or Trendyol Go, the order is read from your e-mail or from a screenshot you share. The items wait for your approval, and the ones you approve go into stock with their prices.

Spending and prices

See what you spent this week or this month, by category. On the 15th, it compares with the first 15 days of last month.

Every purchase keeps its price and store, so you can see how much olive oil went up in a year and which store had it cheapest. The chart also works in euro or dollar.

Other features

  • A notification when something is about to expire or has run out
  • Recipes from what you have, and a weekly dinner plan
  • Clothes and furniture, and a reminder before an appliance's warranty ends
  • Ask "Do I have pasta at home?" and get the answer from your own list
  • Home screen widget and app shortcuts
  • Password-protected backups to a folder you pick

Privacy

Your records stay on your phone and you don't need an account. I don't run a server. The database is encrypted, and a copied file won't open on another phone. When you use an AI feature, the photo or text is sent to Google Gemini to be read.

The privacy policy lists exactly what goes where.
